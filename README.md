# Babili

**An ultra-simple communication tool to help families stay connected.**

Designed for elderly people and individuals with hearing difficulties, Babili provides an accessible and stress-free way to communicate with loved ones.

## Overview

Babili is a web application designed to simplify communication between family members and relatives who may find modern messaging applications too complex or inaccessible.

The platform focuses on simplicity, readability, and ease of use, making digital communication available to everyone regardless of technical skills.

## Features

- Simple and intuitive interface
- Designed for elderly users
- Suitable for people with hearing impairments
- Large, readable interface
- Easy family communication
- Minimal learning curve
- Accessible from standard web browsers

## Why Babili?

Many communication applications are overloaded with features and can be difficult for seniors to use. Babili focuses on what matters most:

- Staying connected
- Reducing digital barriers
- Encouraging regular communication
- Supporting family relationships

## Typical Use Cases

### Family Communication

Children and grandchildren can easily stay in touch with older relatives.

### Assisted Living

Caregivers and family members can maintain regular contact with residents.

### Hearing-Impaired Users

A text-first communication approach reduces difficulties associated with phone conversations.
